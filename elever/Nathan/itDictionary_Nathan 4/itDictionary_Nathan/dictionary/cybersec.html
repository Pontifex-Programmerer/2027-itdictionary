Cyber security

Cyber security helps individuals and organisations reduce the risk and impact of cyber attacks. Its core function is to defend the digital services and devices we rely on from online threats, which includes safeguarding the vast amounts of data and personal information stored locally or in the cloud. Cyber security also ensures that innovative and emerging technologies (such as AI) can deployed in a secure way, so the opportunities they present can be fully realised.

Generative AI

Artificial intelligence capable of generating new things using data and information already existing in its database. Can imitate how humans think and write. Frequently used tools In IT to generate code, discover problems and more. The term is often used on chatbots like Claude or Chat GPT These are language models trained on large amounts of information and the internet. These receive prompts and do tasks.

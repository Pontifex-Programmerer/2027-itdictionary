Navigation

Finding a way from one place to another. Originally referring to the act of navigating a vehicle such as a ship or a car. Frequently used term in IT and computers describing the act of moving around on a website or a computer screen. Web-devolopers often have navigation in mind when making a website or a program. They want to make it as easy, efficent, and fast to navigate on the screen as possible.

Layout

Layout is how something is arranged and where elements are placed. The word layout is used about many things such as the layout of buildings, rooms or gardens or the layout of a poster. The term is often used in design and development. For example, on a website where different elements such as text, images and menus are placed. A good layout can make a website easy to navigate but also aesthetically pleasing.

(Collins Dictionary, u.å.)

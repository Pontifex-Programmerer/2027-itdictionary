Viruses

Viruses are a type of malicious software that spreads by infecting other files or programs. They can cause damage to systems and data, and are often spread through email attachments, malicious websites, or infected software downloads. viruses can be used to steal sensitive information, disrupt operations, or gain unauthorized access to systems. They can also be used to create botnets, which are networks of infected computers that can be controlled remotely by attackers.
